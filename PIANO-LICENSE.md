# Piano sample attribution

HMPS includes five recordings from **Salamander Grand Piano v3**, by **Alexander Holm**,
licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Source distribution: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
Pinned source revision: 3382bf9496bba2486f5ab0de55a264d1dfc38404
Original collection: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
FLAC distribution/remapping: kinwie.

Included velocity-layer-8 recordings: C4, D#4, F#4, A4 and C5.
The FLAC files are unchanged; D# and F# filenames are renamed Ds and Fs.
At runtime HMPS uses at most six seconds, skips a short quiet lead-in, applies
an end fade and release envelope, and pitch-shifts adjacent notes.
This is a compact single-velocity piano, not the full multi-layer instrument.
No endorsement by the sample author is implied.

Assets/Piano/manifest.json records the source URL and SHA-256 checksum of each file.
Keep this notice with redistributed HMPS source, application and instrument builds.
The sample license is separate from the software/dependency licenses.
